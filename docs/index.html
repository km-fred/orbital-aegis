01About the game

Alien fleets converge on your station along glowing energy lanes. You build towers on the hex slots beside those lanes, upgrade them through four tiers, and at level 4 choose which of two specialized weapons each tower becomes — Railgun Sniper or Pulse Gatling, Siege Mortar or Tesla Coil, Cryo Lance or Graviton Well.

Enemies fight back. Towers have energy, take damage, and can be destroyed; you can repair them mid-wave or sell them back for part of their cost. Clear a wave and every surviving tower recovers half its energy. The more towers you build, the harder the enemy pushes — a threat model that scales with your defenses rather than a fixed difficulty curve.

Commander abilities give you two buttons that matter: an orbital strike you aim by hand, and a stasis pulse that freezes the entire field for three seconds. Supply comets drop gold if you tap them. Elite enemies glow gold and pay double.

There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no purchases. All progress is stored on the device, and deleting the app deletes it.

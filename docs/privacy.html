Privacy Policy

Orbital Aegis has no account, no server, and no analytics. Your progress is stored on your device. The only outside party involved is the advertising network that shows the one ad on app launch.

Last updated 1 November 2026 · Applies to Orbital Aegis 1.0 for iOS

The short version

  • No account, no sign-in. The game never asks for your name, email address, or a password.
  • Progress never leaves your device. Stars, unlocks, and your star wallet are written to local storage on the phone. There is no server to send them to.
  • One ad network, one ad. A single app-open ad is shown when the app launches, served by Google AdMob.
  • No tracking. The app does not access the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not ask to track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No analytics, no crash reporting, no social SDKs. Nothing else is bundled in.

01Information collected through the game

The game itself collects nothing. It contains no analytics library, no crash-reporting service, no attribution SDK, and no social login. There is no server component, so gameplay data has nowhere to go even in principle.

The only data that leaves your device is what Google AdMob collects in order to serve and measure the app-open ad. Three categories:

  • Device identifier An identifier AdMob uses for frequency capping and aggregate reporting
  • Advertising data Which ad was shown, and whether it was tapped
  • Product interaction That the app was launched, and limited in-app interaction signals

All three are used for one purpose: third-party advertising. None of them is used for tracking, none is linked to an identity, and none is combined with data from other companies' apps or websites.

02What is stored on your device

Everything below is written to iOS local storage (the app's UserDefaults) and never transmitted. Deleting the app deletes all of it, permanently and irreversibly — there is no cloud backup and no way for us to restore it.

  • Map progressYour best star rating (0–3) for each of the 70 maps
  • Star walletAccumulated stars, items owned, per-run item limits
  • SettingsSound on or off
  • Ad launch countA timestamp used to skip the ad on your very first launch
Nothing here is personal data. It is a list of numbers describing how far you have got in a game. It stays on the phone.

03What is never collected

For clarity, the app does not collect, request, or have access to any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or postal address
  • Any account, login, or password
  • Precise or coarse location
  • Contacts, calendar, photos, camera, or microphone
  • Health or fitness data
  • Payment information — the game has no in-app purchases
  • Your browsing history, search history, or activity in other apps
  • Any content you create

04Third parties

Google AdMob (Google LLC) serves the app-open ad. Google receives the three data categories described in section 01 and may process them on servers outside your country of residence. Google's handling of that data is governed by its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

We do not sell data, do not share it with any other party, and have no ability to read what AdMob collects.

05Tracking

Orbital Aegis does not track you. Specifically:

  • The app never calls the App Tracking Transparency prompt, because it does not need to.
  • The advertising identifier (IDFA) is not accessed.
  • Ads are therefore not personalized based on your activity across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No cross-app or cross-site profile is built, by us or on our behalf.

06Your choices

There is very little to opt out of, because there is very little collected — but you have real controls:

  • Delete the app. All local progress and settings are removed with it. This is immediate and complete.
  • Limit ad personalization at the OS level. iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising controls personalized ads delivered by Apple's own systems. Because this app does not request tracking permission, its ads are already non-personalized.
  • Opt out of personalized advertising from Google at adssettings.google.com.
  • Play without ads is not currently offered. The app-open ad is the only monetization in the game.

07Children

Orbital Aegis is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children — the game has no account system and no way for a user to submit personal information. The ads served are non-personalized. If you believe a child's data has somehow been collected through the app, contact us and we will investigate.

08Data retention

We retain nothing, because we receive nothing. Local game data lives on your device until you delete the app. Ad data is retained by Google according to Google's own retention policies, which we do not control.

09Your rights

Privacy laws in some regions (including the GDPR in the EU and UK, and the CCPA in California) give you rights to access, correct, delete, or port your personal data, and to object to its processing. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to retrieve or erase on our side — deleting the app accomplishes the local part in full. For data held by Google, use the Google Privacy Policy link in section 04 or the opt-out in section 06.

If you believe your rights have not been respected, you may contact us using the details below, or lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

10Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes what it collects — for example by adding analytics, an account system, or personalized advertising — this page will be updated before that version ships, and the date at the top will change. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
